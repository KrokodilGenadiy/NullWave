NullWave icon assets

Base design: Material Symbols / Material Design icons by Google.
Upstream: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
License: Apache License 2.0 (material_symbols_APACHE_2_0.txt).

The 48 nw_icon_*.xml vectors were adapted from the Nullwave_old_2 icon
sheet, whose source comments identify Material Symbols Outlined W300
as their base. They are NullWave restylings, not unmodified upstream assets.
The legacy geometry uses stroked paths with butt caps and mitred joins.

Migration changes: resource names prefixed with nw_icon_; Overflow renamed
More; Confirm renamed Check; Back and Forward now auto-mirror in right-to-left
layouts. Transport glyphs retain their playback direction.
The path geometry is unchanged from the legacy files.
